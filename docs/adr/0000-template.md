# NNNN. Title, as a short noun phrase

**Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, business, constitutional? What's the
problem this decision resolves? State this neutrally; don't argue for the
decision yet.

## Decision

What we're doing, stated as a direct claim ("We will..."), not a discussion.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result — both the benefits and the real
costs/trade-offs accepted. An ADR that only lists upsides is incomplete.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it was rejected. Keeps the decision from
looking arbitrary to a future reader who wasn't in the room.
